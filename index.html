1. Introduction

DLC Business Manager is a Windows desktop application that helps businesses manage customers, quotes, invoices, payments, expenses, appointments, recurring services, documents, and business reporting.

DLC Business Manager primarily stores business information locally on the user's Windows device. DLC Media does not operate a cloud database for the application's ordinary customer, financial, scheduling, photograph, or document records.

Limited network communications occur for weather information and Microsoft Store licensing, purchases, and subscription management as described below.

2. Information handled by the application

Depending on the features used, DLC Business Manager may handle:

  • Business names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, websites, branding, tax settings, payment terms, and application settings.
  • Customer names, company names, telephone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, billing addresses, customer numbers, and notes.
  • Quotes, invoices, line items, discounts, taxes, totals, statuses, and related notes.
  • Payment records, including dates, amounts, methods, references, and notes. DLC Business Manager does not directly process or store payment-card credentials.
  • Expenses, vendor information, categories, notes, and user-selected receipt attachments.
  • Jobs, appointments, quote viewings, service dates, start times, durations, addresses, and recurring service agreements.
  • Property photographs selected by the user, together with filenames, captions, categories, file types, sizes, and selections for customer-facing PDFs.
  • Generated quote and report PDFs, document templates, activity history, numbering settings, and locally cached weather information.
  • Backup files and CSV exports created in locations selected by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority and a lawful purpose for information entered into the application.

3. How information is used

Information is handled to provide the application's business-management functions, including:

  • Maintaining business and customer records.
  • Preparing quotes, invoices, reports, and PDF documents.
  • Recording payments and expenses.
  • Scheduling appointments, quote viewings, jobs, and recurring customer agreements.
  • Attaching property photographs and expense receipts to business records.
  • Producing dashboard totals, reports, and financial summaries.
  • Creating, validating, and restoring local backups and exporting supported records to CSV files.
  • Verifying Free and Pro feature entitlement through Microsoft Store.
  • Displaying weather associated with the configured business location.

DLC Media does not use locally stored customer or financial records for behavioral advertising or profiling.

4. Local storage and security

Primary business records are stored in a local SQLite database on the user's Windows device. Application-managed property photographs, receipt attachments, generated documents, document templates, and preview files are stored in local application-data folders. Backups, CSV exports, and exported PDFs are stored in destinations selected by the user.

The application does not currently provide application-level encryption for the local database, managed files, backup files, CSV exports, or PDFs. Protection depends on the security of the Windows device, Windows account, selected storage locations, and any external or synchronized storage service chosen by the user.

DLC Business Manager uses database integrity checks, relationship validation, backup validation, and SHA-256 integrity values to help identify damaged or invalid data. Complete backup archives include a manifest with a SHA-256 digest and byte size for every database and managed-file entry. These controls are not encryption and do not guarantee protection against unauthorized access.

Users should protect their Windows accounts, devices, backup destinations, exports, and removable storage and should apply appropriate operating-system security updates.

5. Weather information

DLC Business Manager uses Open-Meteo to provide weather information.

When weather functionality operates, the application sends a business-location query to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Where the configured address begins with a street component containing numbers, the application removes that first component and normally sends the remaining city, region, or country information. After geocoding, the application sends latitude, longitude, unit preferences, and forecast parameters to Open-Meteo.

Open-Meteo may receive ordinary network information, including the device's IP address. Weather responses and the source address used for the request are cached in the local database for a limited period to reduce unnecessary requests. Open-Meteo processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

6. Microsoft Store purchases and subscriptions

DLC Business Manager uses Microsoft Store services to look up monthly and annual subscription products, initiate a purchase requested by the user, verify whether a recognized subscription license is active, read the applicable Store product identifier and subscription expiration date, restore purchases, and open Microsoft account subscription-management controls when requested.

Microsoft may process Microsoft account, device, payment, purchase, network, and licensing information under Microsoft's own privacy practices.

DLC Business Manager receives licensing results needed to decide whether Pro features should be enabled. Customer databases, invoices, property photographs, and other ordinary business records are not transmitted to Microsoft as part of Store license verification. DLC Media does not directly receive or store payment-card credentials through the application.

7. Third-party services and sharing

The audited commercial application does not upload ordinary customer, financial, scheduling, photograph, backup, or document records to a DLC Media server.

No DLC Media advertising, analytics, behavioral-tracking, telemetry, or crash-reporting service is integrated into the audited application build.

Information may be handled by third parties when users deliberately select cloud-synchronized folders, network drives, printers, email applications, external applications, or other services. Any resulting transmission is controlled by the user and is subject to the selected provider's privacy practices.

DLC Media does not sell business or customer information stored in DLC Business Manager.

8. Backups, exports, and restoration

The application provides two manual backup formats:

  • Database-only backup (.dlcbak): contains a consistent SQLite snapshot. This legacy-compatible format does not bundle separately stored property photographs, receipt attachments, generated documents, or document-template assets. Its companion manifest records technical information including creation time, schema version, filename, and SHA-256 integrity value.
  • Complete business backup (.dlcbackup): contains a consistent SQLite snapshot plus application-managed property photographs, receipt attachments, generated business documents, and document-template assets. The portable archive contains an integrity manifest with the path, size, and SHA-256 digest of every included payload file.

Complete backup creation stops and reports an error if a database record refers to a missing or unsafe managed file. Before a complete restore changes current data, the application verifies the archive structure, allow-listed paths, manifest, file sizes, SHA-256 values, database integrity, relationships, and schema compatibility. It creates a complete pre-restore safety backup and attempts to roll back both the database and managed files if restoration fails.

Database-only restoration remains supported for older backups. It validates the selected database and creates a database safety backup before replacing records, but it does not restore managed files.

Neither backup format includes preview caches, user-selected CSV exports, externally exported PDFs, external backup folders, or original source photographs and documents outside the application's managed data directory. Users must preserve those separately when required.

CSV exports cover supported customer, quote, invoice, payment, and expense database records. Backups, CSV exports, and exported PDFs remain in their selected locations until the user deletes them.

9. Data retention and deletion

Business records generally remain on the user's device until they are changed, archived, voided, reset, or otherwise managed through the application. Archiving a customer preserves the customer and related history. Voided or cancelled records may remain for financial history and audit purposes.

The Reset Business Data function requires the user to type RESET before proceeding. It removes the active business profile and supported database records and deletes application-managed receipt attachments, property photographs, generated quote documents, template assets, and quote-preview cache files from the application's data directory.

Reset Business Data does not delete:

  • Original photographs or documents selected from outside the application.
  • Backup files stored in user-selected folders.
  • CSV exports or PDFs exported to user-selected folders.
  • Files copied to cloud storage, network drives, removable media, email applications, or other external destinations.

Those files must be deleted separately by the user. Uninstalling the application may not remove data saved outside the application's managed data directory.

10. User choices and privacy rights

Users can:

  • Review and update business and customer information.
  • Archive and restore customer records.
  • Create either a local database-only backup or a complete business backup and choose its destination.
  • Validate and restore current complete archives and compatible older database-only backups.
  • Export supported business records to CSV files and choose where exported files are saved.
  • Remove a photograph from a quote without deleting the original source file.
  • Reset the active business data after explicit confirmation.

Individuals whose information is entered by a business using DLC Business Manager should generally direct access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability requests to that business. The business using the application controls its locally stored records and is responsible for responding to requests under applicable law.

Requests concerning information actually held by DLC Media may be sent using the contact information below.

11. Children's privacy

DLC Business Manager is business-management software and is not directed to children. Users should not enter children's personal information unless they have appropriate authority and a lawful purpose.

12. International processing

Microsoft and Open-Meteo may process relevant licensing, purchase, network, device, or location-query information outside the user's province, state, or country under their respective privacy practices.

The primary DLC Business Manager database remains on the user's device unless the user deliberately places the database or related files in a synchronized, networked, or externally managed storage location.

13. Changes to this policy

DLC Media may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will display a revised effective date and may be communicated through the application, Microsoft Store listing, or this website.
